HomeTV is one SwiftUI app target plus a Top Shelf extension. Add-ons decide what you can watch, TMDB and Trakt add
to it, and the player shows it. This page maps the code. For how a stream is opened and played, see
[Playback architecture](/developers/playback-architecture/).

## Data sources

### Stremio add-ons

Add-ons are the only source of title metadata and streams, and of every catalog except the rows TMDB supplies
(Explore Channels & Apps, the channel screens, and In Theaters & At Home). The Watch Now catalog rows, the hero,
Search, Browse by Genre and the stream picker read from them.

- **`AddonRegistry`** keeps the installed add-ons in order, with each one's manifest and whether it's enabled. At launch,
  if no add-ons are installed, it installs Cinemeta. Views read `enabledAddons`.
- **`StremioClient`** is an actor that calls the standard add-on endpoints: `manifest`, `catalog`, `meta`, `stream`
  and `subtitles`. Catalog and meta responses are cached for five minutes, and identical requests made at the same
  time share one fetch. That's why the hero and the first catalog row cost a single request.

A title's meta comes from the first enabled add-on that answers. Streams come from every enabled add-on in parallel.

### TMDB

TMDB adds data the add-ons don't have, and supplies a few rows of its own. It never replaces an add-on catalog row
or the streams.

- **`TMDBService`** looks a title up by its IMDb ID and returns an `Enrichment`: logo, backdrop, cast with photos,
  credits, certification, where to watch, trailers and recommendations. It also supplies episode stills and
  runtimes, person pages, the Cast & Crew row in Search, and In Theaters & At Home.
- **`ChannelCatalogService`** supplies the streaming channels: the Explore Channels & Apps row and each channel's
  screen.

On the detail screen, which source a value comes from depends on the field. `MetaDetailViewModel` makes the choice:

| Source | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| TMDB first, then the add-on | Description, genres, year, runtime, cast, directors, recommendations |
| The add-on first, then TMDB | Backdrop, logo, rating (the add-on's IMDb rating) |
| TMDB only | Certification, status, region of origin, original audio, where to watch, the provider badge. Without TMDB, certification shows PG-13 for a movie and TV-MA for a series, region of origin shows United States and original audio shows English. |

Trailers in the Trailers row come from Trailerio when it has any, and from TMDB otherwise.

With no `TMDB_API_KEY`, both services return nothing. The fields in the first two rows fall back to the add-on, the
TMDB-only fields show the defaults above or don't appear, and the TMDB rows are hidden.

### Trakt

**`TraktService`** owns the sign-in, the tokens and in-memory copies of your watched titles, watchlist, playback
progress, Continue Watching and Recently Watched. When you're signed in, it syncs at launch and when the app comes
back to the foreground, so a title finished in Infuse shows as watched when you return. A foreground sync is skipped
if the last one was less than 30 seconds ago. `TraktClient` does the requests. The in-app player scrobbles through
`TraktService`. Infuse scrobbles to Trakt itself; VLC and System Default don't report anything to Trakt.

### Trailers

**`TrailerSource`** finds trailers that can play in the app through the Trailerio add-on, when it's installed. The
Watch Now hero, row previews, detail screens and channel heroes play them. Without Trailerio there's no autoplay.
`TrailerResolver` is the fetch-and-rank part, shared with the Top Shelf extension.

### Other services

- **Skip segments**: `SkipSegmentClient` reads intro, recap and credits times from TheIntroDB and IntroDB.app.
- **Artwork**: `ImageLoader` downsamples each image to the size it's drawn at and keeps the result in memory. Raw
  image bytes are kept in the shared `URLCache` (50 MB in memory, 300 MB on disk), which `HomeTVApp` sets up.

## State

### Services and view models

- **App-wide state** lives in `@Observable @MainActor` singletons, each reached with `.shared`: `AddonRegistry`,
  `TraktService`, `LocalLibrary`, `WatchHistory`, `PlayerPreference`, `PlayerPresenter`, `StreamChoiceStore` and
  `DeepLinkRouter`.
- **Network clients and caches** are actors: `StremioClient`, `TMDBClient`, `TMDBService`, `ChannelCatalogService`,
  `TraktClient`, `ImageLoader` and `SkipSegmentClient`. Their work runs off the main actor and views `await` them.
- **Screen state** lives in `@Observable` view models that a view owns with `@State`, such as `WatchNowViewModel`,
  `MetaDetailModel`, `SearchViewModel`, `ChannelViewModel` and `PlayerModel`. Pure logic, such as
  `MetaDetailViewModel`, `StreamPickerViewModel` and `SearchRanker`, is kept in plain values so it can be unit tested.

The project builds with strict concurrency checking set to complete.

### Trakt or the device

Watched state, progress and the watchlist work without Trakt. `UserLibrary` answers for the library: Trakt when
you're signed in, `LocalLibrary` and `WatchHistory` otherwise.

Progress is the exception. `recordProgress` always saves to `LocalLibrary`, even when you're signed in; the in-app
player also scrobbles the position to Trakt. When you're signed in, `progress` and `resumeSeconds` use the newer of
the local play and Trakt's. A local play still wins if Trakt's is up to 2 minutes later, which covers the delay
before the in-app player's scrobble reaches Trakt. Only the local copy has the exact resume second: when Trakt's play
wins, `resumeSeconds` returns nothing and the resume point comes from Trakt's percentage and the runtime. So when a
resume point looks wrong, check `LocalLibrary` too, even for a signed-in user.

Reading and changing watched state, progress and the
watchlist goes through `UserLibrary`. A few views still check `TraktService.isSignedIn` themselves: Watch Now picks
where Recently Watched comes from, the detail screen picks where a series' episode progress comes from, and Library
syncs with Trakt when it appears.

### Storage keys

Everything below is in `UserDefaults.standard` unless noted.

| Key | Owner | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hometv.addons.v1` | `AddonRegistry` | Installed add-ons, their manifests, order and enabled state |
| `hometv.watchHistory.v1` | `WatchHistory` | Titles played on this device, for Continue Watching and Recently Watched when signed out |
| `hometv.localLibrary.v1` | `LocalLibrary` | Watched titles, progress, resume positions, play activity and the watchlist kept on this device |
| `hometv.streamChoices.v1` | `StreamChoiceStore` | The stream each title last played, for one-click resume from Continue Watching |
| `hometv.defaultPlayer.v1` | `PlayerPreference` | The default player |
| `hometv.skipSegments.v1` | `PlayerPreference` | Whether Skip Intros & Credits is on |
| `hometv.audioChoices.v1` | `AudioChoiceStore` | The last audio track chosen for each show or movie |
| `hometv.audioAdjustments.v1` | `AudioAdjustmentsStore` | Enhance Dialogue and Reduce Loud Sounds |
| `hometv.subtitleChoices.v1` | `SubtitleChoiceStore` | The last subtitle choice for each show or movie |
| `hometv.subtitleDelays.v1` | `SubtitleDelayStore` | Subtitle timing, for each stream |
| `hometv.trakt.username` | `TraktService` | The signed-in Trakt username, shown before the session is checked |
| `hometv.topshelf.trailerioBase.v1` | `TopShelfSharedState` | The Trailerio add-on's base URL. Stored in the `group.com.hometv.HomeTVs` App Group for the Top Shelf extension. |

The Trakt tokens are in the Keychain as a generic password, with service `com.hometv.trakt` and account `tokens`.

## Navigation

### Tabs

`RootTabView` is a `TabView` with the `.sidebarAdaptable` style and four tabs: Search, Watch Now, Library and
Settings. That style keeps every tab mounted, so `RootTabView` tells `WatchNowView` whether it's the selected tab, and
the hero stops its trailer while it's hidden.

### Stacks

Each tab has its own `NavigationStack`. Watch Now's path is a typed array of `WatchNowRoute`:

```swift
enum WatchNowRoute: Hashable {
    case detail(MetaPreview)
    case genre(Genre)
    case channel(StreamingChannel)
}
```

Because the path is a plain array, a deep link can replace the whole stack by assigning to it. Settings has its own
`SettingsRoute` path. Library and Search push `MetaPreview` values with `metaDetailDestinations(selection:)`. These
stacks wrap `MetaDetailView` in `MetaDetailDestination`, which sets `.id(meta.id)` so that swapping one title for
another loads a fresh screen. Two pushes don't use the wrapper and push `MetaDetailView` directly: a related title
from the detail screen, and a title from a person's filmography.

Full-screen covers sit over the stacks:

- **Stream picker**: `streamPickerCover(request:)`, presented from a `StreamRequest`.
- **Player**: `playerCover(at:)`, attached at the root and inside the stream picker. `PlayerPresenter` shows the
  player over the picker when the picker started playback, so Back returns to it, and over the whole app otherwise,
  as when you resume from Continue Watching.
- **Trailer player**: `trailerPlayerCover(request:)`.

The Menu button is Back, handled by each `NavigationStack`.

### Deep links

The app answers to the `hometv` URL scheme, declared in `project.yml`. `HomeTVApp.onOpenURL` passes each link to
`PlaybackReturnCoordinator` first, then to `DeepLinkRouter`.

| Link | Sent by | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hometv://detail?type=<type>&id=<id>&name=<name>` | A Top Shelf item | Selects Watch Now and replaces its stack with that title's detail screen |
| `hometv://playback-done-<token>?…` | Infuse, when playback ends or the player is closed | Marks the episodes Infuse got past as watched and saves a resume point for the last one. No resume point is saved if the link has no position or the runtime isn't known. For a season playlist, nothing is recorded if the returned URL matches none of its episodes. |
| `hometv://playback-error-<token>` | Infuse, when playback fails | Clears the pending launch |

The `<token>` is created for each launch, so another app can't fake a playback result.

`DeepLinkRouter` sets `requestedTab` and `pendingDetail`. `RootTabView` switches to Watch Now, and `WatchNowView`
reads the pending title when it appears, which covers a cold launch, and whenever it changes, which covers a link
that arrives while the app is running. The player's Go to Movie and Go to Show buttons open the detail screen the
same way.

## Top Shelf extension

`HomeTVTopShelf` shows a carousel of popular titles in the Apple TV Home screen's top row when HomeTV is in it. It's a
separate process with a memory limit of about 25 MB, so it fetches only JSON and passes artwork and trailers to tvOS
as URLs.

`ContentProvider` does the work:

1. Fetches Cinemeta's top movies and top series and interleaves them, keeping up to eight.
2. Fetches each title's full metadata and, when Trailerio is installed, its best trailer, all in parallel.
3. Skips titles with no wide artwork, so the carousel can hold fewer than eight, and builds a
   `TVTopShelfCarouselItem` for each of the rest with the artwork, synopsis, genres, runtime, release year, IMDb
   rating, director and cast. The trailer plays muted once the item has focus.
4. Gives each item a `hometv://detail` link for Select and Play.

If nothing loads, it returns no content, and tvOS shows the static Top Shelf image.

The extension can't see the app's `UserDefaults`, so it doesn't know your add-ons. It always reads Cinemeta, which is
public. For trailers it needs one value from the app: `AddonRegistry` writes the enabled Trailerio add-on's base URL
to the App Group through `TopShelfSharedState`, at launch and whenever the add-on list changes, and the extension
reads it from there.

The extension compiles a few of the app's files directly, listed under its target in `project.yml`:
`StremioClient`, the Stremio models, `TrailerResolver` and its models, and `TopShelfSharedState`. Files it compiles
can't depend on anything else in the app.

## From Watch Now to playback

Pressing Play on the Watch Now hero:

```text
Watch Now hero, Play
  │  MetaDetailModel.heroPlaybackRequest
  │  For a series, loads the episode list and picks the episode to play next
  ▼
StreamRequest ──► streamPickerCover ──► StreamPickerView
  │  StremioClient.streams on every enabled add-on, in parallel
  │  Streams are grouped by quality and can be filtered by add-on
  ▼
Select a stream ──► PlaybackSession.launch
  │
  ├─ Default player is HomeTV
  │    Before Select, the stream of the focused row is already being prepared
  │    PlayerPresenter.present(PlayerRequest)
  │    playerCover(at: .picker) ──► PlayerScreen, PlayerModel
  │    PlaybackEngines.make(): KSPlayer on an Apple TV, AVPlayer in the simulator
  │    Progress is saved through UserLibrary and scrobbled to Trakt
  │    Back returns to the stream picker
  │
  └─ Default player is Infuse, VLC or System Default
       PlaybackReturnCoordinator.willLaunch registers the launch for Infuse
       and clears any earlier one for VLC and System Default
       PlayerLauncher builds an infuse://, vlc-x-callback:// or plain media URL
       For a series in Infuse, the rest of the season is sent as a playlist,
       or only the selected episode if the episode list hasn't loaded
       or Infuse refuses the playlist link
       UIApplication.open hands playback to the other app
       Infuse returns hometv://playback-done-<token>
         ──► PlaybackReturnCoordinator updates watched state and the resume point
       The scene becomes active ──► TraktService.refreshLibrary
```

Either way, `StreamChoiceStore` records the stream, so the title's Continue Watching card can play it again without
the picker. The detail screen's Play button and the episode cards start from a `StreamRequest` in the same way.
